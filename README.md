## 🇨🇳 中文

当你明确要用自己即梦（Dreamina / Jimeng）账号里的积分生成或编辑图片、视频时，这条流程负责把事办完：自动准备官方本地客户端、给出授权链接，并在登录成功后自动继续原任务，不需要你复制凭据或回复「登录好了」。它会先查目标模式的实时能力与参数，再补齐真正缺失的信息；按需把附件或画布素材解析成可读路径，追踪排队与进度，生成中断后恢复原任务而不重复生成。完成后为素材命名、直接交付到当前画布，并报告账号剩余积分。只提到即梦、Seedance 等品牌或模型名称时不会消耗你的积分，仍走内置能力。

### ✨ 能做什么

- 自动准备官方本地客户端，返回绝对路径并检查帮助、版本与账户状态
- 展示授权链接与必要的用户代码，登录成功后自动继续，不要求用户回报状态
- 每个生成任务先取目标模式的实时帮助，再核对所选模型的参数要求
- 附件与画布素材自动解析成本地路径，无需手工上传或手写路径
- 追踪排队与进度，中断后恢复原任务，不重复生成、不擅自付费重试
- 完成后命名素材、交付到当前画布，并报告剩余积分

## 🇬🇧 English

When you explicitly want to use the credits in your own Dreamina (Jimeng) account to generate or edit images and video, this workflow handles the mechanics: it prepares the official local client, gives you the authorisation link, and continues your original request automatically once login succeeds - no credential copying and no reply of "I'm logged in" needed. It checks the live capabilities and parameters of the target mode first, fills in only the genuinely missing information, resolves attachments or canvas assets into readable paths, and tracks queueing and progress, resuming an interrupted job instead of regenerating it. Finished assets are named, delivered straight to the current canvas, and remaining credits are reported. Merely mentioning a brand or model name never spends your credits - the built-in route is used instead.

### ✨ What it does

- Prepares the official local client automatically, returns its absolute path and checks help, version and account state
- Shows the authorisation link and the required user code, then continues automatically after login - no status reply needed
- Fetches live help for the target mode first, then verifies the parameters of the chosen model
- Resolves attachments and canvas assets into local paths, with no manual upload or path typing
- Tracks queueing and progress and resumes an interrupted job without regenerating or paying for extra retries
- Names the finished assets, delivers them to the current canvas and reports remaining credits
